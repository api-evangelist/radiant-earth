Radiant Earth , a nonprofit technology company supporting open machine learning technologies for Earth science, today released a white paper exploring a data ethics framework for farmers to achieve data ownership . Emerging technologies, such as those combining artificial intelligence and Earth observation satellite data, are essential to “smart farming” innovations. They provide the necessary information to develop agricultural monitoring systems such as cropland and crop type mapping.
