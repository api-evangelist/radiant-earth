Meet Muhamed Tuo We are excited to introduce Muhamed Tuo , a data scientist and a member of the winning team of the AgriFieldNet India Challenge . This competition aimed to classify crop types in agricultural fields across Northern India and was hosted on Zindi . It was organized with a grant from the Enabling Crop Analytics at Scale (ECAAS) Initiative, which is funded by Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and implemented by Tetra Tech .
