Meet Gina Trapani We are excited to have Gina Trapani join the Radiant Earth board of directors, bringing a wealth of experience and expertise from her career in the technology industry. Gina is currently the CEO at Postlight, a leading digital strategy and software engineering firm in New York City. She is also an accomplished writer who has published pieces in the Harvard Business Review, The New York Times, and Wired Magazine.
