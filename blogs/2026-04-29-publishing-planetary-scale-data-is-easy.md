By Jed Sundwall, Executive Director of Radiant Earth Last week, Taylor Geospatial announced availability of Fields of the World (FTW) global field boundaries , a data product comprised of agricultural field boundaries at unprecedented scale: 3.17 billion fields identified in 2024 and 2025 across 241 countries and territories. It’s a phenomenal data product, the result of years of rigorous research and an extraordinary collaboration across industry, academia, and philanthropy. Publishing it was easy.
