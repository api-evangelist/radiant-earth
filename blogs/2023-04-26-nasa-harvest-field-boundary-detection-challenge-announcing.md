Food insecurity is a significant challenge in Africa, affecting millions of people across the continent. According to the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) , an estimated 282 million people in Africa were affected by hunger in 2020, up from 236 million in 2019. Smallholder farmers are the backbone of agriculture in many low- and middle-income countries, and their livelihoods depend on their ability to maximize yields and efficiently use resources.
