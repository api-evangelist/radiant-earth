We are happy to announce that Radiant Earth has received funding from Splunk , a Cisco company. This funding accelerates our work on the Cloud-Native Geospatial Forum (CNG) which aims to empower communities everywhere by making geospatial data more accessible, usable, and beneficial. The support from Splunk allows us to create free content such as our recent webinars to help women advance their careers in geospatial data science and to showcase African use cases of geospatial data .
